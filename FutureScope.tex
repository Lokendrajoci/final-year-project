\chapter*{FUTURE ENHANCEMENT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{FUTURE ENHANCEMENT}

The proposed system establishes a foundation for intelligent Nepali writing assistance and opens opportunities for several future enhancements. While the current implementation focuses on sentiment analysis, contextual spelling correction, and next-word prediction, future extensions can improve personalization, linguistic intelligence, and deployment capabilities.

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{Grammar Error Detection and Correction}  

Future work may extend the current spelling correction framework toward full grammatical error correction. This enhancement could identify sentence-level issues such as agreement errors, word order inconsistencies, punctuation misuse, and contextual grammar mistakes to provide more comprehensive writing assistance.

\vspace{0.5em}

\item \textbf{Cross-Script and Transliteration Support}  

Future versions could strengthen support for mixed-script communication by integrating advanced Romanized Nepali understanding and transliteration capabilities. This would enable smoother interaction for users who frequently switch between Devanagari and Roman script while typing.

\vspace{0.5em}

\item \textbf{Multimodal Sentiment Understanding}  

Future research may integrate additional modalities such as emojis, metadata, voice input, and contextual interaction signals to improve sentiment understanding and produce more accurate emotional interpretation.
\end{enumerate}

Overall, these future directions demonstrate the potential to transform the proposed framework into a comprehensive Nepali language intelligence platform capable of supporting education, communication, and real-world digital interaction.
